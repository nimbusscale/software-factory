Each of these three limits is a requirement with its own FR ID, written with "only", then what it rules out: the app books rooms, not equipment; it serves this office only, not the other offices; and it runs only in a web browser, not as a phone app. The way "The shop shall take orders only from US addresses, not from other countries" is written is the model. A limit written only as what the app refuses, such as "shall not", fails.
