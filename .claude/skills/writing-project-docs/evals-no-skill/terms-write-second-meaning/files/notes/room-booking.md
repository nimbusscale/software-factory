# Room booking app: notes

These are notes from a planning meeting for a small app that lets people in our office book
meeting rooms. Nothing is built yet.

Anyone with an account can book. We call them members. Rooms are booked in half-hour slots,
starting on the hour or the half hour. You can book up to two weeks ahead, never more.

The main screen shows each room's day, with the free slots in green. A member picks a free slot and
books it. A slot that someone else has booked isn't free, so it can't be picked.

Booking is free. We don't charge for rooms, and the app never asks for payment. Some people
assumed we'd charge for the big boardroom, but every room is free for members to book.

Whoever books a slot is that booking's organizer. The organizer can cancel the booking any time
before it starts, and its slot is free again.
