# Waitlists: notes

Notes from a planning meeting about adding waitlists to the room booking app.

When a member wants a room for a time slot that's already booked, the app offers to put them on a
waitlist for that room and slot. A waitlist keeps its members in the order they joined.

If the booking is cancelled, the app books the room for the first member on the waitlist, and
emails them the room and slot.

Waitlists close shortly before the slot starts, so nobody is handed a room at the last minute.
