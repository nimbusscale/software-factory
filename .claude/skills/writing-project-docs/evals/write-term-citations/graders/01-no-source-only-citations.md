No term in the Terms section cites a requirement only to say where the thing comes from, such as the requirement that creates the thing or makes someone it. For example, an Organizer term that cites the requirement making the booking member its organizer fails this. A term may cite a requirement when the term's meaning depends on that requirement's rule.
